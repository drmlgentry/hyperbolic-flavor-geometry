31 → χ₅ : The prime 31 is singled out by a single arithmetic identity:

     a₃₁² − 4 × 31 = 7² − 124 = −75 = −3 × 5²

Here a₃₁ = 7 is the Frobenius trace of the elliptic curve X₀(11) at the prime 31. The discriminant −75 = −3 × 25 lies in −3ℤ², which means the Frobenius eigenvalues at 31 are already defined over ℚ(√−3) — the base field of our Bianchi modular form. This allows the order-5 Dirichlet character χ₅ of conductor 31 to twist the base form at level 283 down to level 283 × 31 = 8773, rather than the usual 283 × 31².

No other tower prime p_k satisfies this condition. It was checked for p ∈ {11, 41, 61, 101, 151, 211, 281}: none have a_p² − 4p in −3ℤ². The prime 31 is the unique solution.
