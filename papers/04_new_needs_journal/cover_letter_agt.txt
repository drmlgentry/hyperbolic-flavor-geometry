Dear Editors,

I am submitting "The Meyerhoff Manifold as a Dual Dehn Filling: Arithmetic Independence of SU(2) and SU(4) Cusped Parents" for consideration at Algebraic & Geometric Topology.

The paper proves that the Meyerhoff manifold arises as a Dehn filling of two arithmetically independent cusped parents (m003 and m019), establishes that their cusp fields are linearly disjoint over ℚ with Galois closure S₄ × ℤ/2 of order 48, proves an analogous disjointness result for a third field pair with Galois closure D₆, and reports a systematic negative-result search for a dual filling of a companion manifold. This work is part of the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry program, though every result in this paper is purely mathematical and requires no physical interpretation.

All computations are reproducible via SnapPy and Sage at 300-bit precision, with scripts archived at https://github.com/drmlgentry/hyperbolic-flavor-geometry.
