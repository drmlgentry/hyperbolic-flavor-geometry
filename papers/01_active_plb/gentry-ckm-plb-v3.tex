\title{Quark Mixing from Hyperbolic Geometry:\\
The CKM Matrix from a Compact Arithmetic 3-Manifold}

\author[1]{Marvin L. Gentry}
\affil[1]{Independent Researcher, Seattle, WA, USA;

ORCID: 0009-0006-4550-2663}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We show that the CKM quark mixing matrix is encoded in the holonomy
geometry of the compact arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold
$\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$ (\texttt{OrientableClosedCensus[43]},
volume $2.029$, $H_1=\Zfive$).
The symmetric Gaussian overlap construction with word triple
$\{\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{AbA},\mathrm{AAb}\}$ and smearing parameter
$\sigma = \frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$ achieves Frobenius fitness
$0.016482$ against PDG~2024, with Cabibbo angle error $0.19\%$
and $p<0.002$ against $50{,}000$ Haar-random unitary matrices.
The Jarlskog invariant vanishes ($J=0$) at leading order due to a
homological class collision: $[\mathrm{AbA}]=[\mathrm{AAb}]$ in
$H_1(\MCKM;\ZZ)=\Zfive$.
The invariant trace field of $\MCKM$ is a degree-10 number field
with defining polynomial
$x^{10}-7x^8-4x^7+17x^6+14x^5-18x^4-14x^3+8x^2+3x-1$,
discriminant $-271488204251$, and signature $(8,1)$,
confirmed by SnapPy at 500-bit precision.
Its predominantly real character (8 real places, 1 complex pair)
explains geometrically why the quark sector has suppressed CP
violation relative to the lepton sector.
All six quark mass ratios $m_f/m_e$ are approximated by norms in
$\ZZ[\sqrt{17}]$ to within $5.7\%$ (permutation null-test $p<0.002$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The CKM quark mixing matrix~\cite{CKM} presents a striking
contrast to the PMNS lepton matrix: the CKM angles are small,
CP violation is suppressed, and the pattern is close to the identity.
In the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry (HFG) framework~\cite{Gentry:Unified},
these features are encoded in the geometry of a specific compact
arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold, $\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$.

The present paper has four main results.
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item The symmetric Gaussian overlap construction reproduces the CKM
  matrix with fitness $0.016482$ and Cabibbo angle error $0.19\%$.
\item The Jarlskog invariant $J=0$ follows from the word triple
  geometry, not from any assumption.
\item The invariant trace field of $\MCKM$ is a degree-10 number
  field with signature $(8,1)$, confirmed computationally.
\item The smearing parameter is $\sigma=\frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$
  (exact value derived from the WRT slope norm theorem).
\end{enumerate}

All computations use SnapPy~\cite{SnapPy} at 150-bit precision.

\section{The Manifold}
\label{sec:manifold}

\subsection{Basic invariants}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccccc}
\toprule
Manifold & Census & Slope & Vol & $H_1$ & CS \\
\midrule
$\MCKM$ & 43 & $(-5,2)$ & $2.029$ & $\Zfive$ & $\approx-0.114$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

$\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$ is the Dehn filling of $m006$ (the figure-8
knot sister manifold) with slope $(-5,2)$.
The first homology $H_1 = \Zfive$ is inherited from the cusped parent:
$m006$ already has $H_1(m006) = \Zfive \oplus \ZZ$; the filling
kills the $\ZZ$ factor while preserving the $\Zfive$.
This stands in contrast to $\MPMNS$, where the $\Zfive$ is created
by surgery on $m003$ (whose cusped $H_1 = \ZZ$ has no torsion).

\subsection{Invariant trace field}

\begin{proposition}[CKM invariant trace field]
\label{prop:itf}
The invariant trace field of $\MCKM$ is the number field $K$ with
defining polynomial
\begin{equation}
p(x) = x^{10}-7x^8-4x^7+17x^6+14x^5-18x^4-14x^3+8x^2+3x-1,
\label{eq:itf}
\end{equation}
discriminant $-271488204251 = -11\times239\times103266719$,
signature $(8,1)$, and Galois group $S_{10}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Computed via SnapPy's \texttt{invariant\_trace\_field\_gens()}
and Sage's \texttt{find\_field(500,40,optimize=True)}.
The result is stable at 200, 300, and 500 bits of precision.
Both \texttt{trace\_field\_gens()} and \texttt{invariant\_trace\_field\_gens()}
return the same polynomial, confirming trace field $=$ invariant
trace field.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Earlier papers in the HFG series claimed the invariant trace field
of $\MCKM$ was $\QQ(\sqrt{17})$.
This is incorrect: real quadratic fields have signature $(2,0)$
(zero complex places) and cannot be the invariant trace field
of an arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold, which requires exactly
one complex place.
The correct field is the degree-10 field~\eqref{eq:itf}.
Note that $\QQ(\sqrt{17})$ does not embed in this field.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Geometric CP suppression}

The signature $(8,1)$ of the ITF means 8 of the 9 Archimedean
places are real.
By the MacLachlan--Reid theorem~\cite{MaclachlanReid} (Chapter~3),
holonomy elements with traces in the predominantly real ITF are
predominantly hyperbolic (real eigenvalues) rather than loxodromic
(complex eigenvalues).
Purely hyperbolic holonomy gives zero twist angles and hence
$J_{\mathrm{CKM}} = 0$ at leading order.
This is the geometric explanation of CP suppression in the quark
sector: the degree-10 trace field is ``more real'' (8 real places)
than the PMNS trace field (0 real places, since
$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3}) = \QQ(\sqrt{-3})$ is imaginary quadratic with signature $(0,1)$).

\subsection{The cover prime and manifold uniqueness}

\begin{proposition}[Discriminant and cover prime]
The prime $11 = L_5$ (fifth Lucas number) divides
$\mathrm{disc}(\mathrm{ITF}(\MCKM)) = -11\times239\times103266719$.
\end{proposition}

This connects arithmetically to the topological cover prime:
the degree-5 covering space of $\MPMNS$ introduces torsion prime
$11$~\cite{Gentry:Farey}, and $11$ also ramifies in the ITF of $\MCKM$.
The prime $11 = \det[(-2,3),(-5,2)]$ (the Farey determinant of the
two HFG filling slopes) thus appears in both the topology and the
arithmetic of both manifolds.

A scan of $m006(p,q)$ fillings confirms that $\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$
is the unique filling with both $H_1 = \Zfive$ and ITF signature $(8,1)$:
the next filling $m006(-6,1)$ has signature $(8,2)$ and is not arithmetic.

\section{Mixing Matrix Construction}
\label{sec:mixing}

\subsection{Setup}

For each geodesic word $w$, the holonomy $\rho(w) \in \PSL$ is
computed via SnapPy's \texttt{polished\_holonomy()} at 150-bit precision.
The axis direction $\hat{n}(w) \in S^2$ is extracted from the
Pauli decomposition of $\log\rho(w)$.
The Gaussian overlap kernel with smearing parameter
$\sigma = \frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$ gives
\[
K_{ij} = \exp\!\left(-\frac{\theta_{ij}^2}{2\sigma^2}\right),
\quad
\theta_{ij} = \arccos(\hat{n}_i \cdot \hat{n}_j).
\]
The symmetric matrix $K$ is rendered unitary via QR decomposition.

\begin{remark}[Exact smearing parameter]
The value $\sigma = \frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$ is derived from
the WRT slope norm theorem~\cite{Gentry:WRT}:
$|\mathrm{WRT}_5(\MPMNS)|^2 = 13/5$,
where $13 = N_{\mathbb{Z}[i]}(-2+3i) = (-2)^2+3^2$ is the Gaussian norm
of the PMNS filling slope $(-2,3)$ in $\mathbb{Z}[i]$, and $|H_1|=5$.
The golden ratio approximation $\sigma \approx \frac{3}{2}\log\phi$
($\phi^2 \approx 13/5$ to $0.69\%$) is replaced here by the exact value.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Word triple and results}

\begin{observation}[CKM word triple]
\label{obs:triple}
The word triple $\{\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{AbA},\mathrm{AAb}\}$
gives geodesic axis angles
$\theta_{12}=48.16^\circ$, $\theta_{13}=77.48^\circ$,
$\theta_{23}=68.43^\circ$,
forming a spherical triangle with excess $13.1^\circ \approx \theta_C$
(Cabibbo angle).
With $\sigma=\frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$ and QR orthogonalization:
\end{observation}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Angle & HFG & PDG~2024 \\
\midrule
$\theta_{12}$ (Cabibbo) & $13.03^\circ$ & $13.05^\circ$ ($0.19\%$) \\
$\theta_{13}$ & $0.201^\circ$ & $0.201^\circ$ ($<0.1\%$) \\
$\theta_{23}$ & $2.36^\circ$ & $2.35^\circ$ ($0.4\%$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Frobenius fitness $\mathcal{F} = 0.016482$ against PDG~2024.
An updated triple $\{\mathrm{abbAbbaB},\mathrm{Ab},\mathrm{B}\}$
achieves fitness $0.009078$; the canonical triple is retained here
for consistency with the companion papers~\cite{Gentry:Unified}.

\section{Jarlskog Invariant}
\label{sec:jarlskog}

\begin{theorem}[$J=0$ from homology]
\label{thm:J}
For the word triple $\{\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{AbA},\mathrm{AAb}\}$,
the words $\mathrm{AbA}$ and $\mathrm{AAb}$ represent the same
class $[2] \in H_1(\MCKM;\ZZ) = \Zfive$.
In the QR construction, identical homology classes force the
imaginary part of the relevant $3\times3$ determinant to vanish,
giving $J = 0$ exactly at leading order.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The experimentally measured Jarlskog invariant is $|J|\approx3\times10^{-5}$.
The geometric construction yields $J=0$ at zeroth order; the observed
non-zero value must arise from higher-order corrections (e.g., quaternion
algebra involution or perturbative flavor mixing).
\end{remark}

This provides a geometric explanation of quark CP suppression
independent of the trace field argument.

\section{Quark Mass Encoding}
\label{sec:mass}

The six quark mass ratios $m_f/m_e$ are approximated by norms in
$\ZZ[\sqrt{17}]$ (the ring of integers of $\QQ(\sqrt{17})$,
with norm form $|a^2-17b^2|$):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lllcc}
\toprule
Quark & $m_f/m_e$ (PDG) & Norm witness & Error \\
\midrule
$u$ & $4.24$   & $|2^2 - 17\cdot0^2|=4$          & $5.7\%$ \\
$d$ & $9.14$   & $|3^2 - 17\cdot0^2|=9$          & $1.5\%$ \\
$s$ & $182.78$ & $|14^2-17\cdot1^2|=179$          & $2.1\%$ \\
$c$ & $2495.1$ & $|29^2-17\cdot14^2|=2491$        & $0.17\%$ \\
$b$ & $8180.0$ & $|117^2-17\cdot18^2|=8181$       & $0.012\%$ \\
$t$ & $338082$ & $|139^2-17\cdot145^2|=338104$    & $0.0065\%$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Five of five non-up quarks have witnesses within $2.1\%$.
Permutation null-test $p < 0.002$ against $50{,}000$ random mass ratios.

\begin{remark}[Status of the quark mass ring]
The ring $\ZZ[\sqrt{17}]$ gives empirically excellent witnesses,
but $\QQ(\sqrt{17})$ does not embed in the confirmed degree-10 ITF
$K_{10}$ of $\MCKM$. In fact, the maximal totally real subfield of
$K_{10}$ is $\QQ$ itself, verified by Sage: $K_{10}$ has no proper
totally real subfields of any degree, and its unique subfield
structure consists only of $\QQ$. The algebraic origin of the
$\ZZ[\sqrt{17}]$ norm witnesses therefore lies outside the ITF
and remains an open problem.
\end{remark}

\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:concl}

We have shown that the CKM matrix arises from the holonomy geometry
of $\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$ via the symmetric Gaussian overlap construction
with the exact smearing parameter $\sigma = \frac{3}{2}\log\sqrt{13/5}$.
The Jarlskog invariant $J=0$ follows from homological class collision.
The ITF of $\MCKM$ is a degree-10 field with signature $(8,1)$,
whose predominantly real character geometrically suppresses CP violation.
The prime $11 = L_5$ appears in both the topology (Farey determinant)
and the arithmetic (discriminant of the ITF), connecting both HFG
manifolds through the modular curve $X_0(11)$~\cite{Gentry:X011}.
