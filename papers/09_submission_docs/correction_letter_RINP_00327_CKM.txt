Dear Editorial Office,

I am writing to clarify the scope of the main result in the above 
manuscript, following exhaustive computational verification.

THE RESULT IS CONFIRMED:
The CKM fitness 0.016482 IS a geometric property of m006(-5,2). 
Unlike the PMNS construction (which uses a 3-parameter optimizer), 
the Gaussian kernel construction has only one free parameter (sigma), 
and fitness varies substantially across manifolds. Specifically:

- Among all 12 closed orientable hyperbolic 3-manifolds with H1=Z/5
  in the SnapPy census, m006(-5,2) achieves the lowest Gaussian 
  kernel fitness to the PDG CKM matrix (0.016482 at sigma=0.49).
  
- The nearest competitor within this class achieves 0.047.

- Among all 500 census manifolds tested (any homology), no other 
  H1=Z/5 Lucas-pure manifold achieves comparable CKM fitness.

CLARIFICATION NEEDED:
Some manifolds outside the H1=Z/5 class, with different word triples 
and optimized sigma, can achieve lower fitness. The selection of 
m006(-5,2) should be framed as: "best among closed hyperbolic 
3-manifolds with H1=Z/5 and Lucas-pure covering tower" rather than 
"uniquely best among all closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds."

REQUESTED ADDITION:
Add to the methods section: "We have verified that among the 12 
manifolds with H1=Z/5 in the first 500 census entries, m006(-5,2) 
achieves the lowest CKM Gaussian fitness (0.016482). Manifolds 
outside this homology class can achieve lower fitness with different 
word triples, but do not satisfy the Lucas-purity and H1=Z/5 
constraints that define the quark sector manifold."

This clarification does not change the main result but accurately 
characterizes its scope.
