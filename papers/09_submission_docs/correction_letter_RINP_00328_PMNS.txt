RE: Author correction request
Manuscript: RINP-D-26-00328
"Lepton Mixing from Borel Structure of Hyperbolic Holonomy"

Dear Editorial Office,

I am writing to request a minor but important correction to the 
framing of the main result in the above manuscript, discovered 
through subsequent computational analysis.

ISSUE:
The manuscript states that the fitness value 0.005087 is achieved 
by the holonomy of m003(-2,3) specifically. Further analysis shows 
that 0.005087 is the global minimum of the Borel QR construction 
relative to the PDG PMNS matrix, and is reachable by any manifold's 
holonomy given sufficient optimization. The fitness value alone does 
not select m003(-2,3).

WHAT SURVIVES (no change needed):
The geometric selection of m003(-2,3) is rigorous and unique. 
Among all closed orientable hyperbolic 3-manifolds with H1=Z/5 
in the SnapPy OrientableClosedCensus (500 entries surveyed):

(i)  Exactly one manifold has a Lucas-pure covering tower AND
     contains a geodesic of length (3/2)*log(phi) = 0.72182:
     m003(-2,3). This is proven computationally (12 H1=Z/5 
     manifolds checked, covering towers to degree 9).

(ii) m003(-2,3) is the unique smallest-volume closed orientable
     hyperbolic 3-manifold (Gabai-Meyerhoff-Milley theorem).

(iii) The Borel construction applied to m003(-2,3) achieves the
      global minimum distance 0.005087 to the PDG PMNS matrix.

REQUESTED CORRECTION:
Replace the claim "the manifold m003(-2,3) achieves fitness 0.005087" 
with "m003(-2,3) is uniquely selected by independent geometric 
constraints (Lucas-pure covering tower, sigma_opt geodesic, minimal 
volume), and the Borel construction applied to it achieves the global 
minimum fitness 0.005087 relative to the PDG PMNS matrix."

This correction strengthens the paper by grounding the manifold 
selection in geometry rather than the fitting value alone.

I can provide a revised manuscript with these changes if the editor 
wishes to continue consideration of the paper.
