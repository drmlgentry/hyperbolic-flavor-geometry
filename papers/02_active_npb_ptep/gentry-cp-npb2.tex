\title{CP Violation from Twist Angles of Hyperbolic Holonomy:\\
A Parameter-Free Prediction of the Leptonic CP Phase}

\author[1]{Marvin L. Gentry}
\affil[1]{Independent Researcher, Seattle, WA, USA;
\texttt{drmlgentry@protonmail.com};
ORCID: 0009-0006-4550-2663}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We construct a parameter-free geometric prediction for the leptonic
CP-violating phase from the holonomy of the compact hyperbolic
3-manifold $\MPMNS = m003(-2,3)$ (\texttt{OrientableClosedCensus[1]},
volume $0.9814$, $H_1=\Zfive$).
The twist angles $\varphi(\gamma)=\mathrm{Im}\log\lambda(\gamma)$
(principal branch) of two loxodromic holonomy words yield:
\[
  \delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}
  = \bigl(\pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})\bigr)
    \bmod 360^\circ
  = 195.91^\circ,
\]
numerically consistent with the PDG~2024 best fit $197.0^\circ$
($0.55\%$ discrepancy, no continuously tuned parameters).
The branch choice of the leading $\pi$ is fixed geometrically by
the orientation of the axis triple:
$\det[\hat{n}(\mathrm{aa}),\hat{n}(\mathrm{aaB}),
\hat{n}(\mathrm{baa})]=+0.079>0$.
The manifold's trace field $\KPMNS=\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$
(imaginary quadratic) provides a qualitative geometric distinction
between the large leptonic and suppressed quark-sector CP phases
within the HFG construction.
The prediction is falsifiable by Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The leptonic CP-violating phase $\delta_{\mathrm{PMNS}}$ is the
least well-measured parameter of the Standard Model flavor sector.
The PDG~2024 best fit is $\delta=197.0^\circ$ with a $1\sigma$
uncertainty of approximately $\pm23^\circ$; Hyper-Kamiokande and
DUNE are expected to reduce this to $\pm5^\circ$ precision.
No first-principles derivation of $\delta$ exists.

In this paper we present evidence that $\delta$ is encoded in the
twist angles of the holonomy representation of a single compact
hyperbolic 3-manifold, $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$, with no continuously
tuned parameters.
The word triple used in the formula was fixed independently by the
requirement of minimising the Frobenius distance to the PDG PMNS
mixing matrix under the Borel overlap construction
(Section~\ref{sec:method}); the CP phase formula emerged from that
triple without further adjustment.
The result is part of the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry (HFG)
framework~\cite{Gentry:Unified}, in which the PMNS mixing matrix
and charged lepton masses are also obtained from the geometry of
$\MPMNS$.

All computations use SnapPy~\cite{SnapPy} version 3.3.2
at 150-bit precision.

\section{The Manifold and Its Holonomy}
\label{sec:manifold}

The manifold $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$ has volume $0.9814$,
$H_1=\Zfive$, and Chern-Simons invariant $1/4$.
Its invariant trace field is $\KPMNS=\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$
(imaginary quadratic)~\cite{Chinburg98}, with ring of integers
$\ZZ[\omega]$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$.
The CKM companion manifold $m006(-5,2)$ has trace field
$\QQ(\sqrt{17})$ (real quadratic); since distinct invariant
trace fields imply non-commensurability for arithmetic
hyperbolic 3-manifolds~\cite{MaclachlanReid}, the two
manifolds lie in distinct commensurability classes.

The holonomy representation $\rho:\pi_1(\MPMNS)\to\PSL$ is
computed via SnapPy's \texttt{polished\_holonomy()} at 150-bit
precision.
For a loxodromic element $\gamma\in\pi_1(\MPMNS)$, the eigenvalue
of maximal modulus $\lambda(\gamma)$ satisfies $|\lambda(\gamma)|>1$,
and the \emph{twist angle} is
$\varphi(\gamma)=\mathrm{Im}\log\lambda(\gamma)\in(-\pi,\pi]$,
where the principal branch of the logarithm is used throughout.

The axis direction $\hat{n}(\gamma)\in S^2$ is extracted from
the Pauli decomposition of $\log\rho(\gamma)$:
\[
  \log\rho(\gamma)
  = \tfrac{\eta}{2}I
  + \tfrac{i}{2}(n_x\sigma_x+n_y\sigma_y+n_z\sigma_z),
  \quad
  \hat{n}=\frac{(n_x,n_y,n_z)}{\|(n_x,n_y,n_z)\|},
\]
where $\eta=\ell(\gamma)+i\varphi(\gamma)$ is the complex
translation length.

\section{Word Triple Selection and Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

The word triple $\{\mathrm{aa},\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{baa}\}$
was selected from an exhaustive scan of all primitive words in the
generators $\{a,b\}$ of $\pi_1(\MPMNS)$ up to word length 5
(484 primitive words before conjugacy reduction, yielding 34
distinct geodesic classes).
The selection criterion was minimisation of the Frobenius fitness
$\mathcal{F} = \||Q|-|U_{\mathrm{PMNS}}|_{\mathrm{PDG}}\|_F$
under the Borel (lower-triangular) overlap construction
(the $N$-factor of the Iwasawa decomposition), with the smearing
parameter $\sigma$ optimised over $[0.1,2.0]$ by Nelder--Mead
for each triple.
The triple $\{\mathrm{aa},\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{baa}\}$ achieves
fitness $\mathcal{F}=0.005087$, the minimum within the scanned
ensemble ($p<10^{-4}$ against $50{,}000$ Haar-random unitaries).

The CP phase formula in Theorem~\ref{thm:main} uses the same word
triple but accesses only the $A$-factor (twist angles) of the
Iwasawa decomposition---a strictly separate quantity from the
$N$-factor used for the mixing matrix.
The formula was not selected to match $\delta_{\mathrm{PDG}}$;
it was identified from the word triple already fixed by the
mixing matrix construction.
This independence is what removes any free parameter from the
CP phase prediction.

\section{The CP Phase Formula}
\label{sec:formula}

\begin{theorem}[Geometric CP-phase computation]
\label{thm:main}
Let $\rho$ be the polished holonomy of $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$.
Using the word triple $\{\mathrm{aa},\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{baa}\}$
fixed by the Borel mixing construction (Section~\ref{sec:method}),
the twist-angle combination
\[
  \delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}
  = \bigl(\pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})\bigr)
    \bmod 360^\circ
  = 195.91^\circ
\]
is numerically consistent with the PDG~2024 best fit
$\delta_{\mathrm{PDG}}=197.0^\circ$ (discrepancy $1.09^\circ$,
$0.55\%$).
No continuously tuned parameters enter once the word triple is fixed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Computational verification]
The twist angles from SnapPy \texttt{polished\_holonomy()}
at 150-bit precision, using the principal branch of $\log$:
\[
  \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) = -176.73^\circ,
  \qquad
  \varphi(\mathrm{baa}) = -167.36^\circ.
\]
Then:
\[
  180^\circ + (-176.73^\circ) + (-167.36^\circ)
  = -164.09^\circ
  \equiv 195.91^\circ \pmod{360^\circ}.
\]
The branch choice of the leading $\pi$ is fixed geometrically:
\[
  \det\bigl[\hat{n}(\mathrm{aa}),\,\hat{n}(\mathrm{aaB}),\,
  \hat{n}(\mathrm{baa})\bigr] = +0.079 > 0
\]
(right-handed triple, selecting $+\pi$ rather than $-\pi$).
Reproducible via
\texttt{conda run -n sage python reproduce/cp\_reproduce.py}
at~\cite{GitHub}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The formula is invariant under conjugation of each word in
$\pi_1(\MPMNS)$: conjugate elements share the same eigenvalues
and hence the same twist angles.
The result is also insensitive to the choice of generator set,
as SnapPy's polished holonomy is computed from the geometric
triangulation, not a presentation.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
The word triple $\{\mathrm{aa},\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{baa}\}$
achieves the minimum Frobenius fitness identified in the scanned
ensemble under the Borel PMNS construction~\cite{Gentry:PMNS}.
The CP phase formula uses only the $A$-factor (twist angles)
of the Iwasawa decomposition $\PSL=KAN$, complementing the
$N$-factor (Borel) used for the mixing matrix.
\end{remark}

\section{Geometric Distinction of CP Sectors}
\label{sec:asymmetry}

\begin{proposition}[Trace field dichotomy]
\label{prop:dichotomy}
The PMNS and CKM manifolds have distinct invariant trace fields:
$\KPMNS=\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$ (imaginary quadratic, discriminant $-3$)
and $K_{\mathrm{CKM}}=\QQ(\sqrt{17})$
(real quadratic, discriminant $+17$).
For arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifolds, the sign of the discriminant
determines whether holonomy elements are generically twisted
loxodromic ($\mathrm{disc}<0$) or purely hyperbolic
($\mathrm{disc}>0$)~\cite{MaclachlanReid}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Verification]
For $\MPMNS$: established in~\cite{Chinburg98}.
For $M_{\mathrm{CKM}}=m006(-5,2)$:
$\mathrm{tr}(\rho(aa))=3-\sqrt{17}$,
satisfying $x^2-6x-8=0$ (discriminant $68=4\cdot17$),
verified at 150-bit precision.
\end{proof}

Within the HFG construction, this arithmetic distinction provides
a qualitative geometric account of the large leptonic CP phase
($\delta=195.91^\circ$, twisted loxodromic holonomy) versus the
suppressed quark CP violation (purely hyperbolic holonomy,
geometric $J=0$ at zeroth order).
The observed non-zero quark Jarlskog invariant
($J_\mathrm{exp}\approx3\times10^{-5}$) requires corrections
beyond the zeroth-order construction.

\section{Comparison with PDG and Experimental Status}
\label{sec:comparison}

The PDG~2024 fit gives $\delta_{\mathrm{PDG}}=197^\circ$ with
$1\sigma$ range approximately $[174^\circ,220^\circ]$.
The geometric prediction $\delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}=195.91^\circ$
lies within the $1\sigma$ range and differs from the central value
by $1.09^\circ$ ($0.55\%$).
A uniformly random phase in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$ falls within
$1.09^\circ$ of the PDG central value with probability
$2\times1.09/360 \approx 0.006$, providing a simple measure of
the significance of the agreement independent of the geometric
construction.

\section{Falsifiability}
\label{sec:predictions}

The prediction $\delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}=195.91^\circ$ is falsifiable:
\begin{enumerate}[(1)]
\item \textbf{Hyper-Kamiokande} will measure $\delta$ to
  $\sim5^\circ$ precision.
  A value outside $[180^\circ,215^\circ]$ at $3\sigma$ would
  falsify Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\item \textbf{DUNE} provides an independent measurement with
  comparable precision.
\item The formula uses words $\mathrm{aaB}$ and $\mathrm{baa}$
  from the triple fixed by the Borel mixing construction.
  If a different triple were found to give better PMNS fitness,
  the CP phase formula would need to be re-evaluated.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Open problems.}
The quark CP phase $\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}}$ from the HFG
construction gives $72^\circ$ (PDG: $68^\circ$, $5.9\%$),
consistent at geometric precision.
The mechanism producing the non-zero $J_\mathrm{exp}$ from the
geometric zeroth-order $J=0$ remains the most pressing open
problem in the framework.

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We have computed the leptonic CP-violating phase from the
twist angles of the holonomy of $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$:
$\delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}=195.91^\circ$, numerically consistent
with PDG~2024 ($0.55\%$ discrepancy, no continuously tuned
parameters).
The branch choice is fixed geometrically by the orientation of
the axis triple.
The result is one component of the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry
framework~\cite{Gentry:Unified}; the same manifold encodes the
PMNS mixing matrix and charged lepton mass ratios.
The imaginary/real trace field dichotomy
(Proposition~\ref{prop:dichotomy}) provides a qualitative
geometric account of the CP asymmetry between sectors.
The prediction is falsifiable by Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE.
