construction in the program, where does phi (the golden ratio) actually appear as an algebraic
invariant of a cusp shape or trace field, versus where it appears only as the base of a numerical
log-lattice fit? Verified against `HFG-CORPUS/database/results.json`, not recollection.

### Selection theorem
Among the 134 census manifolds (of 11,031) with H1=Z/5, M_CKM is the *unique* one with ITF
signature (8,1) -- R-002. This, not any fitting procedure, is the actual "no free parameters"
selection claim.

### The point of the table
Every discriminant across every field above -- cusp shapes and trace fields alike -- is -3, -59,
-283, or -23 (K10's discriminant factors as 11 x 239 x 103,266,719, per R-030's confirmation that
5 does NOT divide it). None is divisible by 5. **None of these fields can contain Q(sqrt(5)) as a
subfield, algebraically, in any construction in the program.** Phi never appears as an invariant
of a cusp or a trace field anywhere in HFG.

## Part 2 — The Lucas/geodesic side, same treatment

Four genuinely different claims get discussed as if they were one. They are not:

1. **Proved, but not manifold-specific.** For any hyperbolic element with eigenvalue phi^k,
   |tr(gamma)| = 2cosh(k*log(phi)) equals the integer Lucas number L_k when k is even, and
   sqrt(5)*F_k (Fibonacci) when k is odd. Pure Binet-formula algebra, true for any manifold. Says
   nothing yet about M_PMNS or M_CKM specifically.

2. **Live, uncorrected error.** CORE_MASTER_v12 Section 7 still states this identity as a blanket
   "= L_k for all k" with no parity split -- mathematically wrong for odd k (verified numerically
   2026-07-30: k=1 gives phi + 1/phi = 2.236, not L_1 = 1). Not fixed as of this writing. See
   `latticefit/results/fermion_tower/` correction log and `MASTER_GAP_REPORT.md`.

3. **Already self-corrected within the corpus.** The "prime dictionary {2,3,7,11,29}" claim -- v12
   itself flags that an earlier version conflated genuine algebraic covers with mere census-volume
   commensurability coincidences. The corrected version: the only verified algebraic cover prime
   through degree 6 is 11 = L_5, via the Farey formula p_n = 5n(n+1)+1 at n=1.

4. **Computational pattern, explicitly still conjectural.** "Lucas-purity" -- the observation that
   M_PMNS's and M_CKM's covering towers only ever produce Lucas-number torsion primes -- is
   verified by finite computation (through degree 6-9) and stated in the corpus itself as a
   conjecture for the general case, not a theorem.

### One genuine exception, stated precisely
Phi/sqrt(5) DOES appear algebraically in exactly one place in the entire program: the Hecke
eigenvalue ray structure of the level-283*31 Bianchi newform (the Frobenius/p=31 result, from
`nt-paper/main.tex`), where Q(sqrt(5)) is generated by the newform's own Hecke multipliers. That is
a real, narrow result about a modular form's arithmetic -- not about any manifold's trace field or
cusp field.

Everything else -- the fermion mass lattice (m = m_e * phi^(q/4)), the geodesic-length clustering
-- is a numerical log-lattice fit to data, not an algebraic invariant of anything in Part 1's table.

## Summary statement (for reuse in slides/papers)

Phi is not an algebraic invariant of any cusp shape or trace field in the HFG construction. It is
the base of a numerical log-lattice that the geodesic length spectrum and fermion masses happen to
cluster near, and (separately, narrowly) the field generated by the Hecke multipliers of one
specific Bianchi newform. The manifolds' own defining arithmetic -- cusp shapes and invariant trace
fields alike -- lives in fields of discriminant -3, -59, -283, -23, and the factors of
disc(K_CKM), none of which involve 5.
